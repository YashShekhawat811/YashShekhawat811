## 🧠 About Me

I'm **Yash Shekhawat**, a **B.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science** interested in building practical solutions across **Data Engineering, Data Science, and AI Engineering**.

I work primarily with **Python and SQL**, and I enjoy moving through the complete data journey — understanding raw data, cleaning and transforming it, designing analytical structures, exploring patterns, and using machine-learning or AI techniques where they add value.

My projects currently reflect two connected directions:

- **Data Engineering:** SQL, ETL, data warehousing, data modeling, data quality, and analytical data pipelines.

I also have practical exposure to **Flask, REST APIs, and Streamlit** and use them when a project needs an application or interface around the data or model. They are supporting skills rather than the center of my profile.
